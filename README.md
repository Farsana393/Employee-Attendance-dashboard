# Employee-Attendance-dashboard
An Excel dashboard on attendance, leave and lateness for 130 employees, with rule-based policy flags.

# Goal
Spot attendance problems early and flag employees who need follow-up.

# What I built
KPI cards, editable targets (95% attendance, flag below 92%, flag at 10+ late days), a risk-tier score, leave bands and a top 10 late arrivals list for Jan to Aug 2026.

# Key findings
- **Target met, thin margin:** 95.2% against a 95% target, with dips in Jan (94.6%), May (94.8%) and Aug (94.5%).
- **August needs attention:** lowest attendance and highest leave (151 days).
- **Leave is concentrated:** 60 employees (46%) take 9+ days, pointing to a workload check, not a company-wide issue.
- **Lateness is concentrated:** four employees (EMP0011, EMP0053, EMP0003, EMP0089) lead with 11–12 late days, so HR can act individually.
- **Prioritised follow-up:** 16 high-risk employees, led by EMP0041 (score 80), replacing manual month-end reports.
